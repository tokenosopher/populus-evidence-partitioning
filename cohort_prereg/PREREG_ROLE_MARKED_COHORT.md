## 1. Background and motivation

The parent study found that a slot-selective attention-mask regime strongly shifted which solutions training discovered in four-cell shared-genome societies. Nine of ten restricted/global matched pairs differed by at least 20 percentage points at both tested depths. All ten restricted societies attained substantial held-out depth-three performance and became chance-level when communication was severed; six restricted societies, selected post hoc to span the observed performance range, were then subjected to packet-intervention audits on success-conditioned episodes. Nine of ten global twins showed a memorization-without-generalization profile, while one global twin learned a high-performing, communication-dependent but episode-entangled solution. The parent battery's complete preregistered conjunction nevertheless formally failed because restricted-arm median depth-three accuracy was 0.6988 rather than the required 0.70.

The parent intervention jointly changed three ingredients: foreign-evidence access, own-slot role information, and active-context load. The present cohort introduces explicit role markers and a neutral-context arm to decompose those ingredients. The R+ versus G+ contrast equalizes marker availability but still changes both foreign-evidence access and readable-context load; it is therefore not, by itself, a visibility-alone comparison.

The parent paper's post hoc crossover also found that both arms were mask-native, which makes a fresh trained control preferable to another test-time mask manipulation.

As reported in Appendix A of the versioned companion manuscript identified in the header, one restricted/tagged-global pair was subjected to a success-conditioned self/cross packet-transplant matrix. ## 3. Hypotheses

- **H1 — role-marked masking contrast.** With explicit role-marker availability equalized, R+ has higher held-out compositional accuracy than G+. This contrast asks whether the parent masking-regime advantage persists after adding an explicit role cue; by itself it does not separate foreign semantic evidence from readable-context load.
- **H2 — global marker effect and operational role rescue.** G+ outperforms G−. The behavioral contrast establishes an effect of the marker intervention in the globally visible regime. The stronger description "operational role rescue" additionally requires the preregistered G+ marker-permutation audit (§5.4) to pass.
- **H3 — restricted-arm marker redundancy.** R+ and R− satisfy the practical-redundancy criterion in §5.3. This tests whether explicit markers materially alter the restricted regime, where slot ownership is already implicit in the mask.
- **H4 — neutral-context exposure.** R+ outperforming N+ under the directional gate supports a detrimental effect of exposing length-matched neutral foreign context relative to masking it. Satisfaction of the practical-null criterion supports no material effect at the frozen resolution. The reverse direction is reported as such. Because the arms differ in readable neutral content as well as effective attention load, this is not described as a pure computational-load manipulation.
- **H5 — task-relevant foreign-content contrast.** N+ outperforming G+ supports a detrimental effect of replacing matched neutral foreign context with task-relevant foreign evidence under explicit markers. The claim is relative to the implemented neutral-filler control and does not establish that every form of foreign semantic information is harmful.

**Role-marker × masking interaction.** At each depth and for every matched trajectory, we report I_d = [A_d(G+) − A_d(G−)] − [A_d(R+) − A_d(R−)], where A_d(X) is final-checkpoint held-out accuracy for condition X at depth d. This measures whether the marker intervention has a larger effect in the global regime than in the restricted regime. It is a preregistered derived estimand, not a seventh trained comparison and not an independent success gate. The phrase "global-specific role rescue" is used only with reference to C2, the marker-following audit, C3, and this interaction jointly. We also report the exact per-trajectory decomposition A_d(R+) − A_d(G+) = [A_d(R+) − A_d(N+)] + [A_d(N+) − A_d(G+)]; medians do not generally add, so the decomposition is reported at the trajectory and initialization-mean levels.

**Prior stance.** The tag-scout provides weak qualitative prior evidence that the tag-format intervention can sometimes alter the global optimization basin: one of three tagged-global trajectories attained communication-dependent map-novel depth-three generalization. That trajectory nevertheless had a severe depth-two inversion, two of three tagged-global trajectories failed, and no causal role-marker-following audit was completed. The pilot therefore raises the plausibility of an occasional marker effect but does not support a directional cohort-level prediction or quantitative prior odds. H1 and H2 are not mutually exclusive, and no ordering of their effect magnitudes is preregistered. The cohort is confirmatory for the frozen gate-defined contrasts and descriptive or estimative for phenotype frequencies.

## 4. Design

Five trained conditions on ONE fresh sealed world; all societies trained from scratch for exactly 20,000 updates under the parent battery's architecture, packet width, normalization, optimizer, and budget. Matched-twin discipline: within a matched set, initialization bytes and the underlying semantic episode order are identical; only the condition factors differ. Condition inputs necessarily differ in serialized tokens; both the common semantic episode stream and every condition-specific serialized stream are hashed and reported.

| # | Condition | Mask | Role marker |
|---|-----------|------|-------------|
| 1 | R− | restricted | absent (neutral placeholder token) |
| 2 | R+ | restricted | present |
| 3 | G− | global | absent (neutral placeholder token) |
| 4 | G+ | global | present |
| 5 | N+ | own span readable + token-matched neutral filler spans visible | present |

**Marker implementation.** For each cell-specific serialization, exactly one slot header carries the fixed `mine:` marker and the remaining three carry `other:`. Marker-absent arms carry the fixed `slot:` placeholder in all four corresponding header positions. The tokenizer IDs, encoded lengths, padding, and final fixed header width are verified and deposited; every condition has identical header-token counts and positions. If the three strings do not tokenize to equal lengths, fixed neutral padding is used to equalize them. In R+, the `mine:` header inside the cell's assigned readable slot is visible and the three foreign headers are masked together with their foreign spans. In G+ and N+, all four headers are readable. Markers introduce no parameters and there are no cell-identity embeddings.

**Neutral-filler construction (N+).** Each cell receives its genuine own evidence span and three deterministic neutral filler spans in place of the foreign evidence. Each filler occupies exactly the token positions of the replaced span and matches the replaced span's READABLE token length exactly: a filler for a span with L readable tokens contains exactly L active tokens followed by the standard padding, under the unchanged span attention mask. N+ serializations therefore carry masks identical to G+ serializations and differ only in foreign content token identities. Fillers are drawn from frozen per-length banks; assignment uses a counter-based pseudorandom function SHA-256(filler_seed : world_id : stream_id : episode_ordinal : slot_id), where stream_id is a fixed nonsemantic bank label (train, monitor, primary, tag, mechanism) preventing schedule aliasing between banks whose ordinals each begin at zero whose inputs exclude every semantic field (operator identities, start values, answers, phrasing rotations); the same slot filler is shared by all recipient cells of an episode; the assignment schedule is archived, and a preflight table reports filler-identity counts by depth, answer label, rotation, and slot. The filler schedule never perturbs the common semantic sampler. Fillers contain no task operators, arithmetic expressions, start values, answer-label tokens, slot numbers, second-person language, or episode-dependent information. The filler generator, deny-list, generated per-length banks, and leakage audit are frozen and hashed before training. N+ therefore estimates the effect of exposing matched neutral context relative to masking; it is not described as a perfectly pure compute-load intervention. Fillers remain recognizable as non-task text; this salience limitation is registered in §10.

**Fresh-world language.** All evidence-span templates — not only operator templates — use neutral third-person constructions. The value span is "The private note for this slot: the starting number is {x}."; the structural-forwarder span is "This slot holds no operation. The received number is passed onward unchanged."; and all operator templates are slot- or position-centered third-person forms. No evidence span addresses the current reader as "you" or "your". The shared question is carried forward unchanged because it is already third-person.

**Fresh-world generation and sealing.** The world-generation code, grammar generator, neutral-filler generator, split constructor, evaluation-bank constructor, tag-audit constructor, phenotype classifier, evaluators, and verdict script are included in the preregistration commit and identified by SHA-256 before any seed is generated; the literal per-file hashes are recorded in cohort/FREEZE_HASHES.json, committed in this same revision, and the freeze commit SHA binds this document to that inventory. World, split, grammar, filler, model-initialization, and data-order seeds are then generated by the frozen deterministic seed rule and committed in a pre-run manifest before any training begins. The first generated world satisfying the preregistered structural validity checks is binding. Permitted rejection reasons are limited to mechanically testable failures specified in the generator — such as duplicate primitive maps, an identity primitive, insufficient collision-free held-out programs, tokenizer failure, or violation of the explicit bank-disjointness checks (primary-pool programs versus training programs and versus diagnostic programs), every one of which is an explicit component of the world-validity conjunction rather than an assertion. Development-exposed seeds are recorded per component role (operator, split, grammar, filler, order, initialization) in the machine-readable exposure manifest `manifests/development_seed_exposures.json`, the single source of truth from which the generator derives its denial rule at import time; a frozen regression test asserts the code-consumed denylist equals the manifest aggregate. The frozen orchestration derives the COMPLETE seed bundle (operator, split, grammar, filler, all order and initialization seeds, and the world identifier) for each candidate counter value and rejects the whole bundle if any component is development-exposed in its corresponding role (comparison is role-specific; numeric equality across roles does not reject; synthetic world identifiers used in unit probes are also denied); rejected bundles are ledgered as development-exposed and the counter increments — a bundle is never accepted with improvised per-field replacements. The world-validity conjunction additionally requires that every operator appearing in the primary pools belongs to the single-operation training curriculum and that every non-identity tag-audit permutation has a nonzero discriminating denominator over the capped program set; a world failing either is rejected mechanically.

**Collision-free primary pool (exact construction).** The depth-two pool is the union of the held-out pair set and the held-out-function pairs; the depth-three pool is the union of the familiar-subchain held-out triples, the held-out-subchain-only triples, and the held-out-function triples; each pool is first-seen-deduplicated and sorted into a frozen deterministic order. The training function set used for collision filtering contains the identity map, every single-operation primitive map, and the composite map of every depth-two and depth-three training program. A pool program is collision-free when its complete composite map is absent from that training function set. The primary evaluation bank enumerates every collision-free pool program at both depths, all 17 start values, and all four sealed phrasing rotations. In-run monitoring programs come exclusively from the diagnostic banks rendered exclusively with qualification phrasings; primary and tag-audit programs come from the held-out non-diagnostic pools rendered with sealed phrasings. Every rejected candidate seed and failed check is retained in the public ledger. No candidate may be rejected on the basis of perceived difficulty, linguistic appearance, pilot-model performance, or any trained-model output. Before training begins, the pre-run manifest records the operator table, split and grammar hashes, serialized condition configurations, common semantic-stream hashes, condition-specific serialized-stream hashes, final-evaluation-bank hashes, monitoring-bank hash, tag-audit-bank hash, evaluator hashes, verdict-script hash, selected execution tier, initialization/order map, and exact primary-bank sizes. Checkpoint hashes are subsequently added to a separate append-only artifact ledger; they do not alter the frozen pre-run manifest. **Primary evaluation uses only the step-20,000 final checkpoint. There is no best-checkpoint selection.** Parent-battery evaluation structure (held-out ordered programs, held-out phrasings, final checkpoints, exact task-bank definitions) is carried forward literally except where this document lists an explicit intentional change (neutralized value, forwarder, and operator language; collision-free primary bank drawn from the held-out pools; four sealed phrasing rotations at every primary, single-operation, and tag-audit evaluation).

## 5. Preregistered contrasts, endpoints, and gates

### 5.1 Signed contrasts (frozen)

- C1(d) = A_d(R+) − A_d(G+) — primary role-marked masking contrast.
- C2(d) = A_d(G+) − A_d(G−) — global marker effect.
- C3(d) = A_d(R+) − A_d(R−) — restricted-arm marker effect (redundancy question).
- C4(d) = A_d(R+) − A_d(N+) — neutral-context exposure contrast.
- C5(d) = A_d(N+) − A_d(G+) — task-relevant foreign-content contrast.
- C6(d) = A_d(R−) − A_d(G−) — fresh-world replication of the parent masking contrast under the revised neutral grammar and collision-free bank.

The predesignated advantaged arms for the directional gates are respectively R+, G+, R+, N+, and R− for C1, C2, C4, C5, C6. **Direction is never changed after outcome inspection.** C3 is a redundancy/equivalence question and has no predesignated advantaged arm. Gate thresholds are cohort-specific directional gates, adapted from the parent battery's thresholds (not a mirror of the parent verdict script, which used 8/10 and a both-depth absolute median floor).

### 5.2 Directional complete-pass gate (C1, C2, C4, C5, C6)

**Fixed design denominator and missingness.** Throughout §§5.2–5.4, n means the design denominator fixed by the committed execution tier: n=12 for GOLD and MINIMUM-DECISIVE, and n=6 for STANDARD. It is never recomputed from the number of available or successfully completed trajectories. If any designated trajectory lacks a valid step-20,000 final checkpoint or required evaluation, every affected complete-pass or practical-null verdict is reported as INCOMPLETE and is ineligible to pass until that trajectory is validly resumed or rerun under the sealed seeds. Available outcomes may be summarized descriptively, but the n−1 count, median gates, and arm-level marker-use gate are never recalculated on a reduced denominator. This same fixed n applies to all directional gates, the C3/C4/C5 practical-null criteria, and the G+ marker-use arm gate. A comparison receives a complete directional pass only if all of the following hold:
1. at least n−1 of n matched trajectories have signed differences of at least 0.20 at both depth two and depth three;
2. the median signed paired difference is at least 0.25 at depth two and at least 0.25 at depth three;
3. the predesignated advantaged arm has median depth-three accuracy of at least 0.70;
4. no trajectory in the predesignated advantaged arm has accuracy below 0.40 at **either** tested depth;
5. at least n−1 of n trajectories in the predesignated advantaged arm have all-packets-cut depth-three accuracy no greater than 0.11.

All-cut outcomes are nevertheless reported for every trajectory in every arm. A comparison passes only on the complete conjunction. If its effect-size components pass but its absolute-performance or communication component fails, that decomposition is reported explicitly rather than collapsed into an undifferentiated failure. The 0.70 floor applies to the predesignated advantaged arm of any directional comparison labelled a complete pass. **The primary behavioral verdict is determined by C1.** The stronger claim that the masking contrast persists after demonstrated functional use of the explicit role cue requires both `C1_BEHAVIORAL_PASS` and `GPLUS_TAG_USE_PASS`, and is reported separately as `PRIMARY_ROLE_EQUALIZED_PASS`. Secondary causal contrasts are still estimated and reported when their floors miss (e.g., a large but incomplete role rescue is reported as such, not as "no role effect").

### 5.3 Practical-redundancy criterion for C3

R+ and R− are described as practically redundant only if: both arms have median depth-three accuracy of at least 0.70; neither arm contains a run below 0.40 at either depth; at least n−1 of n matched trajectories have |C3(2)| ≤ 0.20 and |C3(3)| ≤ 0.20; and the median absolute paired difference is no greater than 0.10 at each depth. This is a frozen practical-null criterion, not a formal equivalence test. If the competence conditions fail, the result is "uninformative about redundancy." If the bounded-difference conditions fail, the signed effect is reported without claiming equivalence. The same numerical practical-null bounds apply separately to C4 and C5. `C4_PRACTICAL_NULL` requires the four conditions above with R+ and N+ as the two competent arms and |C4(d)| as the paired difference. `C5_PRACTICAL_NULL` requires them with N+ and G+ and |C5(d)|. A failed superiority gate is not evidence for either practical null; a practical-null verdict is available only when its complete competence and bounded-difference conjunction passes. These are resolution-bounded descriptive criteria, not formal equivalence tests. C4/C5 practical-null results support "no material contrast at the frozen resolution," not equivalence or proof of no effect. Because C1 is the trajectory-wise sum of C4 and C5, two individually small component contrasts do not mathematically guarantee a small aggregate contrast under these tolerances; the preregistered per-trajectory decomposition makes any such case transparent.

### 5.4 Causal role-marker-following audit (mandatory manipulation check; G+)

The mandatory manipulation check is conducted on G+ under its native global-visibility regime using a sealed depth-three permutation bank. The start-value slot remains fixed. For the three operator cells, the assignment of `mine:` markers to the three operator spans is changed by a non-identity bijection while: leaving all evidence text, token positions, attention masks, packet topology, model weights, and execution order unchanged; preserving, among the three operator-span headers, exactly one `mine:` marker and two `other:` markers in each operator cell's readable input (the start-value header remains fixed as `other:` for those operator cells); and using every predeclared non-identity operator-slot permutation. The expected counterfactual answer is recomputed from the operator sequence implied by the permuted marker assignments. The tag-audit candidate set comprises the first min(24, N_cf3) collision-free depth-three programs under the frozen deterministic program order, with the exact selected count and program-list hash recorded in the populated manifest. For each selected program, the constructor enumerates all five non-identity operator-slot permutations, all 17 start values, and all four sealed phrasing rotations. Cases whose intact and permutation-implied answer labels coincide are removed mechanically before evaluation and counted by program and permutation. Intact accuracy and counterfactual tracking are each calculated separately for every one of the five permutations; the model-level values used by the gate are the unweighted means of the five permutation-specific fidelities. Every permutation must have a nonzero frozen denominator. Exact denominators and per-permutation results are reported. No claim of exact post-exclusion balance across programs or start values is made.

Every audit item has a paired intact-marker serialization and permuted-marker serialization with identical evidence text, program, start value, phrasing, token positions, and mask. Intact-marker accuracy is the fraction of the complete sealed discriminating bank answered with the intact target. Counterfactual composition tracking is the fraction of that same complete bank answered with the permutation-implied target under the permuted markers. Neither quantity is conditioned on intact correctness, and no model-specific case filtering is permitted. The same denominator is used for every G+ trajectory; any mechanically invalid bank item must have been removed by the frozen preflight rather than after model evaluation.

For each G+ model, both intact-marker accuracy and permuted-marker counterfactual-following fidelity are reported. A model counts as causally marker-following only if intact-marker accuracy is at least 0.90 and counterfactual composition tracking under the marker permutation is at least 0.90. Marker deletion, replacement by placeholders, duplication of `mine:`, or removal of all markers does not satisfy this criterion. The G+ arm-level manipulation check passes only if at least n−1 of n G+ trajectories satisfy the per-model criterion. Models are never filtered or reanalysed as a selected "tag-using subgroup" for the primary comparison.

If the arm-level manipulation check fails, C1 remains a valid behavioral comparison between marker-present masking regimes, but it is not described as showing that a gap persists after functional role equalization. A G+ generalization failure under a failed manipulation check is reported as unresolved with respect to semantic role rescue, not as evidence that usable role information would be ineffective. If the manipulation check passes but C2 fails, the result is reported as no gate-level role rescue despite demonstrated causal marker following. For R+, moving the marker to a foreign slot while retaining the restricted mask is not a valid tag-use assay; for N+, marker movement into a neutral filler slot lacks a clean operator-defined counterfactual. Any R+ or N+ marker tests are descriptive only.

## 6. Non-gating secondary analyses

### 6.1 Mechanism battery

The native-mask mechanism battery and its scoring equations are frozen in the deposited evaluator specification. Two constructions are stated literally here. **Approximately norm-matched noise:** direction Z ~ standard normal drawn on CPU in float32 from a torch Generator seeded by the first 8 big-endian bytes (mod 2^63) of SHA-256("noise:v1:<world_id>:<bank_index>:<interface>") — the recipient identity is deliberately omitted so the same direction is reused across recipients for comparability; the direction is scaled to the recipient's intact packet Frobenius norm at that interface, then cast to the execution device; a zero-norm intact packet yields a zero noise packet and is counted; a zero-norm or non-finite sampled direction is an error. **Counterfactual evidence-span rewrite (matched):** with F_i the affine operator prefix map through the sender at interface i and v_i = F_i(x), the packet-counterfactual target is v_i^cf = (v_i + 3) mod 17 and the matched text control rewrites the start-value span to x_i^cf = F_i^{-1}(v_i^cf) (every primitive is a bijection over Z17), retaining the original operators and sealed phrasings, with all packets generated natively; the evaluator asserts F_i(x_i^cf) = v_i^cf and that the rewritten episode's final target equals the suffix-implied packet-counterfactual target, aborting the record on any failure. At interface 0 this reduces to x+3; at later interfaces it generally does not. With no post-outcome additions, it comprises: the intact baseline; all-packets cut; interface-specific packet deletion; approximately norm-matched packet noise; clean interface-specific cross-example derangement using natural wrong-value donors; natural same-value transplantation; fixed-offset counterfactual-value transplantation scored against the suffix-implied target; and the corresponding counterfactual evidence-span rewrite control. Donor selection, value balancing, intervention interfaces, random seeds, and denominator rules are fixed before training. Transplant and destructive-intervention estimates disclose their intact-success-conditioned denominators; the primary all-cut gate in §5 remains a separate full-bank endpoint.

The mechanism-audit sample is fixed before training and never selected by final accuracy, phenotype, monitoring trajectory, or packet behavior. Every R+/G+ trajectory is audited because it already enters the full dialect matrix in §6.2. For R−, G−, and N+, the audit sample consists of the lower-numbered of the two precommitted data-order seeds for every initialization; the exact resulting trajectory IDs are written to the pre-run manifest before training. Each audit begins from a fixed sealed episode bank and reports its initial bank size, intact accuracy, success-conditioned denominator, interface-specific denominator, donor availability, and every exclusion. No audit bank is adaptively enlarged after observing a low denominator. An intervention estimate with fewer than 30 eligible recipient cases is labelled low-denominator descriptive evidence and cannot support an interface-type claim.

### 6.2 Cross-regime dialect audit (full self/cross matrix)

For every matched R+/G+ twin pair: the complete 2×2 donor–recipient transplant matrix at all three inter-cell interfaces, all 17 values, both diagnostic phrasing rotations; per recipient–interface, the same base-correct episodes for both donors; conditions = same-value preservation, fixed-offset counterfactual following (scored against the suffix-implied target), shifted-value retention, random mismatched-value retention, and zero-packet deletion; donor episode metadata and per-case predictions persisted; donor–recipient exact-overlap audited by (program, start value, phrasing). Donor packets are harvested under the donor model's native trained regime, and every recipient is evaluated under the recipient model's native trained regime; cross-regime transfer changes the packet donor only and does not override the recipient's mask.

For condition k, donor D, recipient R, and interface i, the recipient-normalized cross-compatibility ratio is ρ_k(D→R, i) = p_k(D→R, i) / p_k(R→R, i). The raw numerator and recipient-self denominator are always reported. Ratios are not clipped. If the recipient-self denominator is zero, the ratio is reported as undefined rather than replaced by zero or infinity. Preservation and counterfactual-following ratios are reported separately. These ratios are descriptive normalizations only and do not identify a donor-side or recipient-side causal contribution.

Four preregistered descriptive indicators are reported at the recipient–interface level: strong within-regime canonicality, partial within-regime canonicality, the operational context-entanglement indicator, and cross-regime interoperability. Their operational definitions are stated literally in §9 of this document and implemented by the frozen aggregator scripts/cohort_dialect_aggregate.py, SHA-256 0942208fb5fc381c627c158950ad6207b3d0d884c34cb5278b10b00d5b147c1d. The indicators are not assumed mutually exclusive; no mutually exclusive summary table will be produced unless its priority hierarchy is added to this preregistration before freeze. Inclusion or omission of the optional R−/G− dialect extension is fixed in the pre-run manifest before training begins and cannot depend on behavioral, monitoring, or packet-audit outcomes. If omitted, no R−/G− dialect conclusion is drawn.

### 6.3 Monitoring disclosure

A fixed liveness bank, generated and hashed before training, is disjoint from every primary final-evaluation, tag-audit, and dialect-audit case. It may be evaluated every 500 updates. Liveness outputs affect no loss, schedule, stopping rule, checkpoint choice, rerun decision, arm choice, tier choice, audit selection, or protocol amendment. Infrastructure intervention is permitted only upon objective logged failures such as process termination, non-finite state, checksum mismatch, or hardware loss. All monitoring reads and interventions are recorded. The untouched primary final bank is first evaluated only after all designated step-20,000 checkpoints for the selected tier have completed.

## 7. Tier, sample, and stopping rules

**Tier commitment.** The execution tier is selected once and committed publicly **before any fresh-world, initialization, or data-order seed is generated.** No tier downgrade, arm removal, initialization removal, or conversion to the minimum design is permitted after seed generation, training, monitoring, or evaluation has begun. If resources are insufficient for the committed tier, launch is postponed rather than reduced after partial observation.

- **GOLD:** twelve matched trajectories per comparison, six initialization clusters, 60 societies (≈$135–170 at the measured 6.0 h/society basis with 1.3 buffer). [Pre-freeze amendment 2026-09-12: sixth initialization added per referee recommendation, before any seed generation.]
- **STANDARD:** six matched trajectories per comparison, three initialization clusters, 30 societies.
- **MINIMUM DECISIVE:** twelve R+/G+ matched trajectories, six initialization clusters, 24 societies; only C1 and its manipulation check remain preregistered cohort endpoints.

Fixed training budget (20,000 updates); no early stopping; no interim efficacy analyses. **Resume precedence:** a run is resumed only from the latest bit-exact verified full-state checkpoint; if no such checkpoint exists, it is rerun from step zero using the original sealed seeds; if duplicate valid finals are inadvertently produced, the first chronologically completed valid final is the designated endpoint and all duplicates are retained and disclosed. Every infrastructure event is logged in the public incident ledger.

**Births×biographies firewall.** Any separately preregistered births×biographies study may reuse the already designated R− checkpoints, but no additional society from that study enters this cohort's denominator, replaces a failed cohort run, changes the audit sample, or informs any cohort gate.

## 8. Statistics

**Statistical unit.** At GOLD and MINIMUM-DECISIVE scale, every two-arm comparison contains n=12 complete matched initialization-by-order trajectories grouped into six initialization strata. At STANDARD scale, n=6 trajectories grouped into three strata. Gate counts and medians use the complete trajectories exactly as preregistered; inferential uncertainty is clustered by initialization.

For each comparison and depth we report: every order-specific matched difference; the trajectory-level median used by the frozen gate; for each initialization, the mean of its two order-specific paired differences; the initialization-level means (six at GOLD/MINIMUM, three at STANDARD); the mean paired difference and a cluster-bootstrap interval obtained by resampling complete initialization blocks (preserving all conditions and both orders jointly; 100,000 draws; seed 271828; interval = percentile 2.5/97.5; script SHA-256 7d8b2c397c89e839135471e677b5bf1865a6b6e9fff0d246319cb0e18a0e99e8, cohort/stats_engine.py); and an exact two-sided sign-flip analysis over the initialization-level mean differences.

With six initialization strata, the smallest attainable exact two-sided sign-flip p-value is 0.03125; with three strata it is 0.25. These tests and cluster-bootstrap intervals are therefore reported as descriptive uncertainty summaries, not as conventional p<0.05 confirmation. All contrasts enabled by the committed tier are reported: all six under GOLD or STANDARD, and C1 alone under MINIMUM-DECISIVE. Intervals and p-values are unadjusted and no familywise-significance claim is made; the preregistered conjunction gates, not null-hypothesis significance testing, determine the comparison verdicts. Tests remain two-sided; no switch to one-sided testing is permitted after observing outcomes. For phenotype frequencies, the raw trajectory counts and initialization-grouped outcomes are reported; no binomial interval treating the two orders as independent is attached. Evaluation episodes estimate within-checkpoint accuracy and are never treated as independent training replicates.

## 9. Integrity machinery

Before contributing any cohort trajectory, every execution machine must reproduce the frozen bit-exact prefix fingerprints for every distinct condition code path specified in the machine-admission fixture and must match the sealed fresh-world fingerprint. The admitted software environment, package lock or container digest, CUDA and driver metadata, precision settings, and hardware identifier are persisted.

Training and evaluation run with deterministic algorithms enforced, TF32 disabled, and the frozen gradient-accumulation and precision configuration. A final checkpoint is admitted only if its internal step is exactly 20,000 and its condition, world, initialization, data-order, semantic-stream, serialized-stream, optimizer-state, and checkpoint hashes agree with the pre-run manifest and artifact ledger.

All final-checkpoint evaluations are performed with the frozen evaluator revision. Evaluation records are hashed as produced. The scientific verdict script may be dry-run before unblinding only on synthetic fixture data with known expected outputs. It is run once on the complete real evaluation set after all designated checkpoint and evaluation artifacts for the committed tier have passed integrity checks. Its command, stdout, structured output, environment record, and hashes are archived.

Any execution outside this procedure, any real-data verdict-script rerun, any post-freeze code change, and any failed integrity check is recorded in the public incident ledger and treated as a deviation rather than silently substituted into the preregistered verdict.

**Machine-admission prefix fingerprint.** ADMISSION_PREFIX3 is recorded after exactly three completed optimizer updates at a zero-pending-gradient accumulation boundary. Its state digest covers all trainable parameters (bridge projections, mouth, auxiliary head, and LoRA), the optimizer state in parameter-group order, the Python data-sampler RNG state, the Torch CPU RNG and every CUDA RNG state, and the optimizer-step counter; the episode ordinal is step-derived (32 episodes per update) and the frozen protocol uses no scheduler object, no gradient scaler, and no NumPy RNG in the training path — declared here explicitly. The accompanying semantic and serialized stream hashes cover the same three-batch prefix. Admission additionally requires the model-level attention-mask test to pass with every acceptance field true: writer invariance at cell 0; reader-path invariance at every downstream cell with the incoming packet held fixed by the frozen substitution convention; incoming-packet sensitivity measured at the writer residual (the pre-anchor per-stage output), because the mailbox anchor's direct residual path would make any final-packet comparison positive by construction; own-span sensitivity; global-arm non-invariance control; and direct cell-level attention-row assertions that question positions are readable in every cell call, both packet pseudo-token positions are readable at every downstream stage (and masked at the no-mail publication stage), and the span segment equals the frozen layout mask row exactly, padding included.

**§6.2 descriptive-indicator definitions (frozen; round-4 amended).** Strong within-regime canonicality: self same-value preservation ≥ 0.90 AND self counterfactual following ≥ 0.90 at the recipient–interface. Partial within-regime canonicality: both ≥ 0.60 and not strong. Operational context-entanglement indicator: self same-value preservation < 0.60 AND deletion ≤ 0.70 on the success-conditioned eligible set (whose intact baseline is 1.000 by construction, i.e. a deletion loss of at least 0.30); this indicator does not uniquely identify context entanglement as the only possible mechanism. Cross-regime interoperability: in BOTH donor directions, all of — recipient-self same-value preservation ≥ 0.60; recipient-self counterfactual following ≥ 0.60; raw cross-donor same-value preservation ≥ 0.50; raw cross-donor counterfactual following ≥ 0.50; recipient-normalized preservation ratio defined and ≥ 0.50; recipient-normalized counterfactual-following ratio defined and ≥ 0.50. Indicators are not mutually exclusive. A stratum with fewer than 30 eligible cases reports raw metrics with status LOW_DENOMINATOR and receives NO positive or negative indicator assignment. A degenerate donor-coverage intersection marks the affected secondary matrix MATRIX_NOT_VIABLE without affecting the primary cohort verdict and without triggering any replacement world, model, or donor-selection rule. The §6.2 common-case rule is implemented by donor-coverage intersection: the frozen coordinator harvests both donors' per-interface value coverage, intersects it, and every matrix cell for a recipient is evaluated under that intersection so self and cross cells share an identical eligible case set, asserted by case-id hash equality before any ratio is formed.

## 10. Residual-confound register (acknowledged in advance)

1. In R+, own-slot information is redundant by construction with the slot-selective mask; in G+, the explicit marker may need to be learned and used to recover comparable role information. This creates an asymmetric learning burden that is measured rather than assumed away.
2. Marker tokens differ lexically from placeholders and may act as attention anchors. The masking, neutral-context, and task-relevant-content contrasts — R+ vs G+, R+ vs N+, and N+ vs G+ — are marker-versus-marker. The marker-effect contrasts necessarily compare markers with placeholders. Causal semantic marker following is directly audited only in G+; C3 remains a behavioral restricted-arm redundancy test because the restricted mask prevents an analogous clean marker-relocation intervention.
3. The tagged cohort is a different world from the parent battery → all comparisons are within-cohort; C6 anchors continuity as a replication under the revised neutral grammar and collision-free bank, not a byte-level reproduction.
4. N+ filler is neutral text rather than task-like distraction. Semantic-conflict distractors are outside this cohort and may be tested separately if N+ does not produce a directional contrast at the frozen resolution. N+ may additionally reveal its own evidence through the contrast between one task-bearing span and three neutral spans; this is registered as a limitation of the N+ vs G+ semantic-content interpretation.
5. See §8: matched trajectories are clustered in six (or three) initialization strata; Gold-60 does not create 60 independent replicates.

## 11. Reporting commitments

The primary behavioral verdict and the role-marker manipulation verdict are reported separately. No comparison direction is reversed after unblinding. A failed superiority gate is never described as equivalence or evidence of no effect. A contrast may be described as a complete pass only if its effect-size, competence, and communication components all pass. "Role rescue" requires demonstrated causal marker following; marker availability alone is insufficient. R+ vs G+ is not called visibility-alone without reference to the N+ decomposition. All comparison components, reverse-direction outcomes, absolute-floor misses, low-denominator audits, and practical-null failures are reported. Pilot, companion-case-study, and cohort data are never pooled. No performance-selected subgroup replaces the intention-to-train arm-level analysis. All contrasts enabled by the committed tier are reported regardless of outcome. The phenotype census accounts for every designated trajectory: each is classified from its valid final or explicitly listed as unavailable. Missingness never reduces a gate denominator.

## 12. Pre-freeze protocol history (development findings; lineage record)

Two implementation defects were found and corrected during the pre-freeze build, both before any seed generation, and are recorded here so the lineage is permanent:

1. **Stale-module world construction (2026-09-11).** The initial world-candidate loop purged the task module from the interpreter's module table but left the parent package attribute alive, so every candidate seed silently rebuilt the same world. Fixed with explicit module-cache isolation; a permanent regression test (different seeds must produce different worlds; a repeated seed must reproduce exactly) is part of the frozen test suite.
2. **Collision-free pool correction (2026-09-11).** The collision-free primary programs were initially drawn from the diagnostic banks (60 depth-three / 12 depth-two programs), under which the frozen ≥20/≥8 minima were mechanically unattainable (0 of 30 fixture worlds valid; observed maximum 13 of 60, consistent with the parent battery's map-novel rate). The pool was corrected to the held-out sets as specified in §4, the collision filter was extended to include the identity and primitive maps per the round-2 build review, and the diagnostic banks were reserved exclusively for in-run monitoring. All fixture seeds used in these investigations are in the development-exposed denylist.

Referee build-review rulings (phase 1, currency sweep, round 2) are archived in the evidence repository alongside this document.

## Appendix A — Phenotype decision tree (frozen with this deposit)

Classification unit: the individual trained trajectory (society), using untouched primary final-bank accuracies at depth two (A2) and depth three (A3); exact full depth-two training-bank accuracy (T2); exact accuracy on the frozen first-150-program depth-three training subset (T3); and held-out-phrasing single-operation accuracy (S1). Training-bank evaluations use the frozen deterministic rendering and are not minibatch training-loss estimates. Define T = max(T2, T3) for the memorizer/non-acquirer split; "memorizer" thus denotes high exact fit to at least one composite training bank while both held-out depths remain below 0.20. S1 is reported descriptively and does not determine class assignment. All-cut results do NOT enter classification; communication dependence is reported alongside. Classes are evaluated in the fixed order below; the first matching class is assigned (tie-breaking by precedence). Thresholds are final.

1. **Full generalizer:** A2 ≥ 0.70 AND A3 ≥ 0.70.
2. **Deep-only generalization inversion:** A3 ≥ 0.40 AND A2 < 0.40 AND A3 − A2 ≥ 0.20. (Behavioral phenotype; no mechanistic claim. Under these thresholds the parent global outlier, A2 = 0.5674 / A3 = 0.8431, classifies as intermediate/mixed, and the pilot GT m200_o950 classifies here as intended.)
3. **Shallow-only partial:** A2 ≥ 0.40 AND A3 < 0.40.
4. **Memorizer:** T ≥ 0.90 AND A2 < 0.20 AND A3 < 0.20.
5. **Non-acquirer:** T < 0.90 AND A2 < 0.20 AND A3 < 0.20.
6. **Intermediate/mixed (residual):** any trajectory matching none of the above.

The classifier script implementing exactly this tree is committed with the freeze and its SHA-256 recorded here: 43d46911c29df0d0eda57ec69e3d9b87701067065511dcd4be48b98e10fb371a (cohort/phenotype_classifier.py). Counts are reported per arm for all trajectories. The phenotype labels are behavioral generalization phenotypes and do not imply communication dependence; all-cut outcomes are reported alongside them. Initialization-level reporting does not collapse two discordant order trajectories into a single class: for each initialization, the ordered pair of its two trajectory classes is reported, together with a 6×6 cross-order concordance table and the number of concordant versus discordant initializations. An initialization receives a single named phenotype only when both orders receive the same class; otherwise it is labelled discordant with both constituent classes shown.
