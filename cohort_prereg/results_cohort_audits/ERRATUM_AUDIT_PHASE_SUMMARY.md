historical narrative written at audit completion. Three of its statements were corrected during
manuscript refereeing and should be read as superseded:

1. "816/816 cases each" (universal): the 816 figure is the initial mechanism-bank episode count

2. "intact = 1.000 (channels load-bearing)": the intact rate is 1.000 by construction

   established by the deletion cell and the behavioral all-cut instead.

   pairs had no interface meeting the preregistered bidirectional interoperability conjunction;
   individual directions can be substantially better than their reverses, and the exceptional
   pair qualifies at its final interface only.
